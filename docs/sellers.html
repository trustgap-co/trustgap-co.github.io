Your workflow

Five steps, all from your phone.

  1. 1. Create the link

    Copy the item and the buyer’s address from your chat. You see the courier price and your exact payout before you send it.

  2. 2. Wait for “Payment secured”

    You get an SMS when the buyer pays. Not a screenshot: a confirmed payment.

  3. 3. Book pickup

    Add a short packing note (what’s in the parcel and its condition). That note is your evidence.

  4. 4. Follow the delivery

    Your dashboard shows the parcel status. The rider confirms delivery with the buyer’s PIN.

  5. 5. Get paid

    When the inspection window ends with no valid problem, your payout goes to your verified account, even if the buyer never replies.

Why buyers trust it

Checks that protect you too.

Identity check (NIN)

We confirm your name against your NIN. Buyers see “Identity verified” on your link. Your NIN and date of birth are never shown to buyers.

Payout account in your name

Payouts only go to a bank account whose name matches yours. Nobody can redirect your money by changing an account number in chat.

A record of every deal

The exact deal the buyer agreed to is locked, with your packing note and the courier’s records, as evidence if a buyer claims something false.

“Identity verified” means we checked who you are. It is not a statement about your products; buyers still get their inspection window.

Fair to sellers when things go wrong

  • Buyer goes quiet? You’re paid automatically when the inspection window ends.
  • Buyer refuses a correct item? It comes back to you, and delivery and return costs can be deducted from their refund, not from you.
  • A false claim? Disputes are decided on evidence: your packing note, the courier’s records and photos from both sides.
