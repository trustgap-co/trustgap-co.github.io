# Website images

Drop these files in this folder with **exactly** these names. Until a file is here, the page shows a
labelled placeholder in its place. All are landscape 4:3 at 1200×900 unless noted, saved as WebP
(under ~200 KB each). JPG works too, but then change the extension in the HTML.

| File | Used on | What to show |
|---|---|---|
| `og-image.jpg` | link previews (WhatsApp, X, etc.) | 1200×630. TrustGap logo on navy + “Agree. Secure. Deliver. Inspect. Release.” |
| `social-commerce.webp` | Home, “Why TrustGap exists” | A small business owner chatting with customers on WhatsApp/Instagram on a phone |
| `buyer.webp` | Home, “For buyers” | A buyer receiving a parcel at the door / checking the item |
| `seller.webp` | Home, “For sellers” | A seller packing an order into a parcel or bag |
| `delivery.webp` | Home, “Delivery” | A dispatch rider handing a parcel to a customer at a gate or door |
| `how-handover.webp` | How it works | A buyer opening a parcel at the door while the rider waits |
| `buyer-phone.webp` | For buyers | A buyer looking calmly at their phone, reviewing a payment link |
| `seller-phone.webp` | For sellers | A seller in their shop or stall sending a link from their phone |
| `seller-handover.webp` | For sellers | A seller handing a packed parcel to a dispatch rider |

Use photos you have the rights to (your own, or licensed stock). Avoid showing other companies'
logos or rider uniforms unless that partner has approved it.
