How TrustGap protects both sides

For buyers

  • You see the exact item, condition, price, delivery fee and rules before you pay. That version is saved and can’t be changed.
  • Your money is held until the item reaches you. You get a 4-digit delivery PIN — only give it to the rider after checking the item.
  • After delivery you have a short inspection window to report hidden problems.
  • Wrong, fake or damaged item? It goes back and you’re refunded to your own bank account.

For sellers

  • Every seller’s identity is checked against their NIN and payouts only go to an account in their own name.
  • You only ship once the buyer’s money is secured.
  • No more buyers refusing to pay at the door: delivery is confirmed with the buyer’s PIN.
  • If the buyer goes quiet after delivery, you are paid automatically when the inspection window ends.
  • Your packing note and the rider’s records are your evidence if there is a dispute.

Fees

Two lines, both shown on the link before anyone pays:

  • TrustGap fee: 1.5% of the item price (minimum ₦100).
  • Payment processing: what Flutterwave charges to take the payment and pay the seller (about 2% plus a small payout fee), passed on at cost.

Together that is roughly 3.9% of the price. The seller and buyer agree who pays it — the buyer, the seller, or half each.
