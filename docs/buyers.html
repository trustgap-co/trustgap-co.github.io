For buyers

“If I transfer first, will the seller disappear?”

With a TrustGap link you don’t have to gamble. You see the whole deal before you pay, the seller isn’t paid until the item reaches you, and there’s a clear way to report a problem.

What you see before you pay

One page with the whole deal.

The seller sends you a link. No app, no account. It shows:

  • The item, its condition and quantity, as the seller described it
  • Your delivery address, so you can catch a mistake before paying
  • The exact total, with every fee on its own line
  • Whether the seller’s identity has been checked
  • When the seller must dispatch and how long you have to inspect
  • The rules: when the seller is paid, and when you are refunded

Report a problem

Your part

Four things to do.

1

Check the link before paying

Item, condition, address and total. If anything is wrong, don’t pay. Ask the seller for a corrected link.

2

Pay only on the TrustGap link

Never transfer to the seller’s personal account “to make it faster”. Outside the link, TrustGap can’t help you.

3

Check the item, then give your PIN

Your 4-digit PIN comes by SMS and on your tracking page. Give it to the rider only after checking the item at the door.

4

Report hidden problems in time

You have after delivery. Use Report a problem with photos or a video.

Honest about limits

A defined process, not a magic guarantee.

TrustGap does not promise that every seller is honest or every item is genuine. What it does is put a process between your money and the seller:

  • The seller isn’t paid until delivery and your inspection window are over
  • Disputes are reviewed by TrustGap staff using evidence from both sides and the courier
  • Refunds go only to an account in your name, which the seller never sees
  • Any deduction from a refund is shown to you in writing

Read the Returns, refunds & disputes policy.

Stay safe from scams

  • TrustGap will never ask for your card PIN, OTP or password by chat, call or email.
  • Real TrustGap links start with trustgap-co.github.io/pay.html.
  • A payment screenshot is not proof. Trust only the status on your tracking page.
  • Never give your delivery PIN to anyone except the rider, at the door, after checking the item.

Buying from someone on WhatsApp?

Ask them: “Can you send me a TrustGap link?” It costs them nothing to sign up, and it protects you both.
