The basics

What is TrustGap?

TrustGap is a Nigerian payment-protection service for buying and selling on WhatsApp, Instagram and other social platforms. The seller sends one TrustGap link; the buyer sees the exact item, photos, delivery address and fees before paying; the payment is secured under the deal rules; a courier delivers; the buyer confirms with a private PIN and has an inspection window; then the seller is paid. If something goes wrong, there is a published dispute and refund process.

How do returns work?

Don’t send anything back on your own; TrustGap arranges every return. If you refuse an item at the door, the same rider takes it straight back to the seller and TrustGap reviews the refusal: a wrong item gets a full refund; a correct item refused is refunded minus both delivery trips. If you find a problem later, report it before the inspection window ends; if TrustGap upholds it, a rider collects the item from you (you have 3 days to hand it over). Either way the seller gets a return PIN and gives it to the rider after checking the item, and your refund is sent once the seller has it back. See How returns work.

How much does TrustGap cost?

The TrustGap fee is 1.5% of the item price up to ₦200,000, then 1% on the rest (minimum ₦100, maximum ₦15,000). Payment processing (the payment provider’s charges, about 2% up to ₦2,000, plus a small payout fee) is passed on at cost. Delivery is booked, tracked and handled by TrustGap and priced for the route. If TrustGap has to decide a dispute, whoever is at fault pays a dispute fee (10% of the item price, between ₦1,000 and ₦3,000). Buyer and seller choose who pays each part: the buyer, the seller, or half each. Every amount is shown before anyone pays. See Fees.

Does TrustGap replace WhatsApp or Instagram?

No. Keep the conversation where it started. TrustGap adds a protected deal link for the payment, delivery and release steps.

Does the seller get my money immediately?

No. The deal page shows the release condition. In the standard flow, the seller is paid after confirmed delivery and the stated inspection window, subject to the published dispute rules.

Do I need an account to buy?

No. Buyers just open the seller’s link, check the deal and pay. You get a tracking link by SMS.

Why does the payment page say “”?

TrustGap is a trading name of , the registered company that runs it. Payments are processed by Flutterwave into that company’s account, so its name appears on the payment page and as the beneficiary for bank transfers. Only pay through the TrustGap link, never to a seller’s personal account.

Is TrustGap an escrow service or a bank?

No. TrustGap is a transaction-coordination service: it records the agreed deal, connects payment and delivery, and applies the release and dispute rules shown on the deal. Payments are processed by a licensed payment provider.

Delivery and inspection

Can I inspect the item before confirming delivery?

Yes. Check the item at the door (right item, model, size, colour, no visible damage), and only then give the rider your 4-digit PIN. After that, you have to report a hidden problem.

When does the inspection window start?

When you give the rider your PIN and the parcel is marked delivered. The deal shows the length of the window before you pay.

What if the item is wrong, damaged, fake or not as described?

Open a dispute within the inspection window and provide the requested evidence. TrustGap then follows the published review, return and refund rules. See Returns, refunds & disputes.

What if I’m not home when the rider comes?

The rider records a failed attempt and the courier contacts you to try again. Your money stays held while this happens.

Can I use TrustGap if the seller is in another state?

Yes. The seller enters their pickup state and your delivery state when creating the link, and the courier’s price for that route is shown on the link before you pay.

Sellers

Does TrustGap verify sellers?

TrustGap checks a seller’s identity (NIN) and that their payout account is in their own name. That does not mean TrustGap has independently verified every item, claim or business practice. Check the deal details and our policies before paying.

When does the seller get paid?

When the release condition is met: the buyer confirms, or the inspection window ends without a valid problem. Payout timing: .

What if the buyer never responds after delivery?

The seller is paid automatically when the inspection window ends.

Can I change a link after sending it?

Links can’t be edited, so the buyer always pays for exactly what they saw. Make a new link and the old one is retired automatically.

Safety and privacy

What information do you collect?

From buyers: name, phone, email, delivery address, and a refund account. From sellers: name, phone, business name, NIN check result, pickup address and payout account. Plus the details of each deal and its delivery. See the Privacy Policy.

Why is a NIN check needed for sellers?

To reduce impersonation and payout fraud: the payout must go to the person who created the link. Buyers see only “Identity verified”, never the NIN or date of birth.

What is shared with the payment and delivery partners?

Only what each needs. The payment provider gets payment and payout details; the courier gets the recipient’s name, phone and address. The seller never sees your refund account.

Does TrustGap store my card or bank login?

No. Card payments happen on the payment provider’s secure page; TrustGap never sees your card number, PIN or OTP. Refund account numbers are stored encrypted.

How long are records kept?

Only as long as needed for the deal, disputes, accounting and the law. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I report a scam or suspicious message?

Email . TrustGap will never ask for your card PIN, OTP or password, and real links start with trustgap-co.github.io.

What happens after a data incident?

We assess the risk and notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and affected people where the law requires. See Data protection & your rights.
