About us

The team closing the trust gap in Nigerian online shopping.

TrustGap is built in Nigeria for the millions of people who buy and sell on WhatsApp and Instagram, and who deserve to know what happens after they click pay.

What TrustGap is

One link for safer social-commerce deals.

TrustGap is a Nigerian payment-protection service for deals made on WhatsApp, Instagram and other social platforms. The seller sends one TrustGap link; the buyer sees the exact item, photos, delivery address and every fee before paying; the payment is secured under the deal rules; a courier delivers the item; the buyer confirms with a private PIN and gets an inspection window; and only then is the seller paid.

Agree. Secure. Deliver. Inspect. Release.

Why we built it

A chat is not a payment guarantee.

Nigerian social commerce runs on conversations, referrals and small businesses. But buyers fear paying and receiving nothing, and sellers fear dispatching and never getting paid. “Send your account details” and a screenshot of a transfer are not enough to protect either side.

TrustGap’s founder had already built a verified property marketplace to replace blind trust with proof. TrustGap brings the same idea to everyday online shopping: the deal is clear, the payment is secured, delivery is tracked, and both sides know what happens next.
