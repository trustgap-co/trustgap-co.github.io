Acceptable Use and Prohibited Items Policy

What you may and may not use TrustGap for.

You may use TrustGap only for lawful transactions

You must use TrustGap honestly, lawfully, and for the purpose for which it is provided. You must not use the Services to:

  • defraud, deceive, impersonate, threaten, harass, or exploit another person;
  • sell stolen, counterfeit, pirated, unlawfully imported, or unauthorised goods;
  • sell weapons, ammunition, explosives, controlled drugs, illegal substances, or other prohibited goods;
  • sell human remains, organs, endangered wildlife, hazardous materials, or unsafe products;
  • sell items requiring a licence or regulatory approval without the required authority;
  • sell financial products, investments, credit, insurance, cryptocurrency, or payment services without the required approval;
  • sell adult sexual services, exploitative material, or any item involving abuse or exploitation;
  • sell medicines, medical devices, supplements, cosmetics, food, or other regulated products in breach of applicable requirements;
  • facilitate money laundering, sanctions evasion, tax evasion, terrorist financing, or proceeds-of-crime transactions;
  • use another person’s bank account, card, identity, phone, or delivery details without authority;
  • create false deals, fake orders, circular transactions, or self-purchases to manipulate records;
  • submit a false dispute, fake evidence, improper chargeback, or refund claim;
  • upload malware, attack the Services, scrape data, probe security, or bypass access controls;
  • send spam, phishing links, or misleading TrustGap-branded messages; or
  • infringe intellectual-property, privacy, confidentiality, or other rights.

TrustGap’s response

We may reject a listing, cancel or pause a deal, suspend an account, withhold or reverse funds where permitted, notify a partner, preserve evidence, report suspected crime, or cooperate with a regulator or law-enforcement body.

Seller responsibility

A seller must confirm that the item is lawful, safe, accurately described, available, and permitted for delivery. TrustGap may require documentation for higher-risk categories.

Questions

Contact before listing an item if you are unsure whether it is permitted.
