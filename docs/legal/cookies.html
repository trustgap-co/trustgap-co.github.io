What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies are small files or identifiers stored on a device or used to recognise a browser, maintain a session, remember preferences, protect security, and understand website use.

How TrustGap uses them

TrustGap may use:

  • Strictly necessary cookies for login, security, session management, fraud prevention, and core functions;
  • Preference cookies to remember choices such as language or display settings;
  • Analytics cookies to understand page performance and usage; and
  • Marketing or advertising cookies only where lawfully permitted and with any consent required.

The exact vendors, cookie names, purposes, durations, and international transfers should be listed in a live cookie register before publication.

Your choices

Where required, the TrustGap cookie banner will allow you to accept, reject, or manage non- essential cookies. You can also change browser settings, but disabling necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Services from working.

Cookie choices do not necessarily delete personal data already processed for a transaction or legal obligation. To request access or deletion, contact .
