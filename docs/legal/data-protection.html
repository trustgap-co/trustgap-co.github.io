Our data-protection commitment

TrustGap is designed to reduce risk in online transactions. That includes protecting the personal data needed to create, pay for, deliver, and resolve a transaction.

We process personal data in accordance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023, applicable directives, and other applicable law. We aim to process data fairly, lawfully, transparently, securely, and only for a clear purpose.

This page is a plain-language summary. Our full Privacy Policy explains the categories of data we collect, our purposes, our partners, retention, transfers, and security practices.

What we need and why

We may need your name, phone number, email, address, transaction information, delivery information, and payment reference so we can provide the TrustGap service. Sellers may also need identity or payout-account verification to reduce impersonation and payment fraud.

We share only the information needed for the relevant task. A courier may need delivery details. A payment provider may need payment and fraud-prevention information. A seller does not automatically receive your identity documents or unrelated account information.

Your rights

You may ask us to explain how we use your data, provide a copy, correct it, delete it where legally possible, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or provide your data in a portable format where applicable.

To make a request, email with “Data Subject Request” in the subject line. We may verify your identity before responding. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you are not satisfied with our response.

Security and incidents

We use access controls, authentication, security monitoring, vendor controls, and other technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the data and the risks. Do not share your login code, payment PIN, OTP, or delivery PIN with anyone who contacts you unexpectedly.

If we identify a personal-data incident that creates a risk to your rights, we will take the steps required by law, including notification where required.

Partner processing

TrustGap may use payment and delivery partners. Flutterwave and Fez Delivery may process data under their own terms and privacy notices as well as the TrustGap arrangements applicable to the transaction. See our Privacy Policy and the relevant partner links before using a partner service.
