Fees shown before payment

TrustGap displays applicable charges before the buyer pays. Depending on the deal, charges may include:

  • TrustGap service fee: trustgap-co.github.io/fees.html;
  • payment-processing or payout fee: trustgap-co.github.io/fees.html;
  • delivery fee: [CALCULATION METHOD];
  • return or redelivery fee: [RULE]; and
  • applicable taxes: [RULE].

The deal page shows who pays each charge. No material charge should be added after payment unless the user agrees or the charge is required by law and properly disclosed.

Payment provider

Payments may be processed by Flutterwave or another provider shown at checkout. The provider may apply authentication, fraud screening, payment-network rules, chargeback rules, settlement delays, or transaction limits.

TrustGap does not ask for card PINs, OTPs, or full security codes by chat, email, or phone.

Use only the official payment page.

Payment status

“Payment initiated”, “payment successful”, “payment secured”, “dispatch authorised”, “delivered”, “inspection open”, “released”, “refunded”, and similar statuses have the meanings displayed in the deal flow. A screenshot is not conclusive proof of payment; rely on the TrustGap status and payment-provider confirmation.
