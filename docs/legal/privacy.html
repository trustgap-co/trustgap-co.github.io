1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how , trading as TrustGap (“TrustGap”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), collects, uses, shares, stores, and protects personal data when you visit our website, create or use a TrustGap account, create or pay for a deal, contact us, use our support channels, or otherwise interact with our services.

TrustGap is registered in Nigeria under number and has its registered address at . You can contact us at or .

This Policy applies to buyers, sellers, visitors, delivery recipients, business contacts, support users, and other individuals whose personal data we process in connection with TrustGap.

TrustGap may act as a data controller for some processing and as a data processor or joint controller for other processing, depending on the service, the parties involved, and the applicable agreement. Our lawyer and data-protection professional should confirm those roles for each material processing activity.

2. What personal data we collect

Depending on how you use TrustGap, we may collect:

  • Identity and account data: full name, business or page name, date of birth where required, username, account identifiers, and identity-verification information.
  • Verification data: NIN-related or other government-issued identification information, verification results, bank-account ownership information, and information needed to prevent impersonation, fraud, or unlawful use. TrustGap should not collect or retain more identity information than it needs for the stated purpose.
  • Contact data: phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, delivery address, landmarks, and other contact details.
  • Transaction data: deal-link details, item description, item condition, price, fees, delivery charge, buyer and seller details, payment status, payout status, dispute status, inspection-window status, return information, and communications relating to the transaction.
  • Payment data: payment status, payment references, method, amount, currency, and limited payment metadata. Payment-card credentials should be handled by the authorised payment provider and should not be stored by TrustGap unless expressly disclosed and lawfully implemented.
  • Delivery data: pickup and delivery address, recipient details, tracking number, delivery status, courier events, delivery confirmation, handover PIN status, proof-of-delivery information, and return information.
  • Communications and support data: messages, calls, support tickets, complaints, evidence submitted for a dispute, and records of our communications with you.
  • Device and technical data: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers, log data, security events, and information about how you use the website or application.
  • Cookie and analytics data: information collected through cookies or similar technologies, subject to our Cookie Policy.
  • Public or third-party information: information you make public on a social-media account or information received from identity, payment, delivery, fraud-prevention, analytics, or other service providers.

Please do not send us sensitive personal data that we do not request. Do not include card PINs, one-time passwords, full payment-card security codes, or account passwords in a chat, support ticket, deal description, or dispute submission.

3. Why we use personal data

We may process personal data for the following purposes:

  1. To provide TrustGap services. This includes creating accounts, generating deal links, recording agreed deal details, accepting or confirming payment, coordinating delivery, displaying status, managing inspection windows, processing payouts, and handling returns, disputes, and refunds.
  2. To perform or take steps toward a contract. We use information needed to provide the service you request or to prepare the relevant buyer, seller, delivery, payment, or business agreement.
  3. To comply with law and regulatory requirements. This may include accounting, tax, anti-fraud, identity, consumer-protection, payment-network, court, law-enforcement, and data-protection requirements.
  4. To protect users and TrustGap. We may detect, prevent, investigate, and respond to fraud, impersonation, unauthorised access, cyber threats, prohibited goods, money laundering, abuse, chargebacks, and other unlawful or harmful activity.
  5. To communicate with you. We send service messages, payment and delivery updates, security alerts, support messages, policy updates, and other communications needed to operate your transaction.
  6. To improve and secure the service. We may use logs, aggregated data, testing information, and service analytics to maintain reliability, improve user experience, investigate errors, and develop features. Where possible, we use aggregated, anonymised, or pseudonymised information.
  7. For legitimate interests. Where permitted by law, we may process data for responsible business administration, service security, fraud prevention, customer support, product improvement, and enforcement of our rights, provided those interests do not override your rights and freedoms.
  8. For marketing. We send promotional communications only where permitted by law and with any consent or opt-out mechanism required. You can opt out using the unsubscribe method in the message or by contacting .

Our legal basis for each material processing activity should be recorded internally and made available in a processing register. Depending on the activity, the basis may be contract, legal obligation, consent, vital interests, public interest, or legitimate interests.

4. Information from sellers and buyers

A seller may provide TrustGap with a buyer’s name, phone number, email address, and delivery information to create or fulfil a deal. A buyer may provide information about a seller or item in a dispute. Each user must have a lawful basis to provide another person’s data and must not provide data that is unnecessary, misleading, or unlawfully obtained.

Where a user submits another person’s information, the submitting user must ensure that the information is accurate and that the person has been appropriately informed where required by law.

5. Who we share personal data with

We may share personal data only as necessary, lawfully, and proportionately with:

  • Payment providers and financial institutions, including Flutterwave or another provider identified on the relevant payment screen, to process payments, payouts, refunds, fraud checks, chargebacks, and payment support.
  • Delivery and logistics providers, including Fez Delivery or another provider identified for the relevant transaction, to collect, transport, track, deliver, return, and support a parcel.
  • Identity, fraud, security, communications, hosting, analytics, customer-support, and technology providers acting under appropriate contractual and security arrangements.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, insurers, and service providers where reasonably necessary and subject to confidentiality obligations.
  • Regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies, tax authorities, payment schemes, or other authorities where required or permitted by law.
  • Business successors or transaction parties in connection with a merger, financing, restructuring, acquisition, or sale of all or part of the business, subject to appropriate safeguards.
  • The other party to a transaction, but only with information necessary to create, pay for, deliver, complete, dispute, return, or support the deal. For example, a delivery provider may need a recipient’s name, phone number, and address; the seller does not automatically need access to every identity-verification document submitted by a buyer.

We do not sell personal data for money. We do not share data for unrelated marketing by third parties unless lawfully permitted and appropriately disclosed.

The specific roles of TrustGap, Flutterwave, Fez Delivery, sellers, and other providers must be confirmed in written agreements. Some providers may issue their own privacy notices and terms, which may also apply to their processing.

6. International transfers

Some service providers may process personal data outside Nigeria. Where personal data is transferred across borders, TrustGap will apply the safeguards required by the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023, applicable directives, and other applicable law. Depending on the circumstances, safeguards may include an adequacy decision, approved transfer mechanism, contractual safeguards, consent, necessity for contract performance, or another lawful basis.

The countries, categories of recipients, and safeguards for material international transfers should be listed in TrustGap’s internal data map and disclosed where required.

7. Security

TrustGap maintains technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, alteration, disclosure, and misuse.

Measures may include access controls, least-privilege access, authentication, encryption in transit, secure development practices, logging, monitoring, backups, vendor controls, staff training, incident response, and data minimisation.

No website, app, payment system, delivery network, or method of transmission is completely secure. You must protect your login codes, one-time passwords, handover PINs, and device. TrustGap will not ask you to disclose a payment PIN or one-time password through an unsolicited message.

8. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain transaction and accounting records, comply with law, prevent fraud, enforce agreements, and establish or defend legal claims.

The retention period depends on the type of data and its purpose. TrustGap should publish or make available a retention schedule covering account records, transaction records, identity-verification records, support records, delivery records, dispute evidence, security logs, and marketing preferences.

When retention is no longer necessary, data will be securely deleted, anonymised, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with our retention policy and applicable law.

9. Your data-protection rights

Subject to legal conditions and limitations, you may have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal data is used;
  • request access to personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • request deletion where there is no lawful reason for continued processing;
  • request restriction of processing in appropriate circumstances;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests or direct marketing;
  • withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis;
  • request data portability where applicable;
  • request human review or additional information about significant automated decisions where applicable; and
  • lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

To exercise a right, contact with the subject “Data Subject Request”. We may need to verify your identity before acting. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and may lawfully refuse or limit a request in certain circumstances.

10. Complaints and breach notifications

If you believe TrustGap has mishandled your personal data, contact first so that we can investigate and respond. You may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission through its official channels.

If TrustGap becomes aware of a personal-data breach, we will assess the risk and make notifications to the Commission and affected individuals where required by applicable law.

The timing, content, and recipients of a notification will depend on the nature and risk of the breach.
