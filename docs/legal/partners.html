Who does what in a TrustGap deal: TrustGap, the payment provider and the courier.

TrustGap connects several parts of a transaction. The parties have different responsibilities.

TrustGap

TrustGap provides the deal link, transaction record, payment and delivery status communication, inspection and dispute workflow, and release process described on the deal page and in the TrustGap policies.

TrustGap is not automatically the seller, manufacturer, authenticator, courier, bank, card issuer, payment processor, insurer, or guarantor.

Flutterwave or other payment provider

The payment provider processes payment and, where applicable, payout, refund, fraud, authentication, or chargeback functions under its own terms and applicable payment- network rules. The payment provider may decline, delay, reverse, or investigate transactions.

Use only the partner language and logo approved in writing by the payment provider. Do not describe the provider as holding TrustGap funds in escrow unless that statement is legally and contractually accurate.

Fez Delivery or other courier

The courier provides pickup, transportation, tracking, delivery, handover, and return services for eligible routes and items under its own terms and operating limits. Delivery times and coverage may vary by route and conditions.

Use only the courier capabilities confirmed for TrustGap’s integration. Do not promise nationwide coverage, live tracking, insurance, locker pickup, or a particular delivery time unless available for that deal.

Third-party terms

Third-party providers may apply their own terms and privacy notices. If there is a conflict between these disclosures and a provider’s terms, the lawyer should confirm the allocation of responsibility and the wording shown at checkout.
