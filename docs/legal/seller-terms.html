1. Seller agreement

These Seller Terms apply when you create or use a TrustGap seller account, create a deal link, list an item, receive a buyer payment, dispatch an item, or receive a payout. They supplement the TrustGap Terms of Service.

2. Seller verification

TrustGap may require the seller’s legal name, business or page name, phone number, email, identity-verification information, payout-account information, and other information needed to reduce fraud and comply with law.

A seller must ensure that the name used for payout matches the account or verification information required by TrustGap. TrustGap may pause a payout where identity, bank ownership, fraud, sanctions, or compliance checks are incomplete or inconsistent.

Verification does not certify product authenticity, quality, availability, seller solvency, or lawful ownership of every item.

3. Listing standards

Every listing must be accurate, current, and sufficiently detailed for a buyer to make an informed decision. A seller must disclose material defects, used or refurbished condition, missing accessories, restrictions, warranty limits, delivery constraints, and any fact likely to affect the buyer’s decision.

A seller must not switch an item, alter its condition, substitute a different model, or change a material deal term after payment without the buyer’s agreement and the process allowed by TrustGap.

4. Dispatch and evidence

A seller must dispatch only after TrustGap shows that the buyer’s payment is secured and dispatch is authorised. The seller should package the item appropriately, follow courier instructions, retain dispatch evidence, and provide requested proof in a dispute.

5. Payout

Payout is made according to the release rule shown on the deal. TrustGap may delay or withhold payout for a dispute, return, chargeback, fraud investigation, payment-provider action, inaccurate bank details, or legal requirement.

6. Seller conduct

A seller must respond to buyer, TrustGap, payment-provider, and courier enquiries within . A seller must honour approved returns, refunds, replacements, and lawful consumer remedies.

7. Seller fees

Seller fees and payout deductions are shown before the deal is created or accepted. A seller must not add an undisclosed charge to a buyer after payment.

8. Seller responsibility

The seller is responsible for the item, listing, price, taxes, licensing, product safety, authenticity, legal title, packaging, and applicable consumer remedies. The seller is also responsible for complying with laws applicable to its business and goods.
