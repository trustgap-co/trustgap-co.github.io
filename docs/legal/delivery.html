1. Scope

This Delivery Policy explains how delivery is arranged for eligible TrustGap deals. The deal page is the primary source for the delivery charge, delivery address, courier, estimated timing, tracking method, inspection window, and return instructions.

TrustGap coordinates the transaction flow. Delivery is performed by the courier identified on the deal, currently intended to include Fez Delivery where available. The courier’s own terms may also apply.

2. Delivery information shown before payment

Before payment, the deal should show:

  • delivery fee and who pays it;
  • delivery origin and destination;
  • expected service area and estimated timing;
  • courier or delivery method;
  • tracking method, where available;
  • pickup, locker, or alternative delivery options, where available;
  • what happens after a failed delivery attempt;
  • return or redelivery charges; and
  • the delivery event that starts the inspection window.

3. Buyer responsibilities

The buyer must provide an accurate address, phone number, name of the recipient, and delivery instructions. The buyer must be reachable and must not knowingly provide an unsafe or false address.

The buyer should check the parcel and item as soon as reasonably possible. The buyer should not disclose the handover PIN before the approved handover step. A PIN is not a replacement for the dispute process where the item is later found to be materially wrong, damaged, counterfeit, incomplete, or not as described.

4. Delivery attempts and exceptions

Delivery may be delayed or fail because of traffic, weather, security restrictions, inaccessible roads, inaccurate addresses, recipient unavailability, phone failure, restricted locations, force majeure, or courier capacity.

If a delivery attempt fails, the courier or TrustGap may contact the buyer and seller to arrange redelivery, pickup, a locker, a corrected address, or a return. Additional charges may apply only if disclosed in the deal or otherwise permitted by law.

5. Loss or damage

Report visible damage or suspected loss through [SUPPORT CHANNEL] as soon as possible and within [TIME]. Keep the packaging and evidence. The applicable courier terms, seller obligations, insurance arrangements, and consumer rights will determine the remedy.

TrustGap does not describe a parcel as insured unless insurance is actually purchased or included for that deal and the coverage, exclusions, claim process, and limits are shown before payment.

6. Tracking and proof of delivery

Tracking updates may be supplied by the courier and may not appear instantly. A tracking event, delivery confirmation, photograph, signature, PIN, GPS event, or courier record may be used as evidence of delivery, subject to correction where the record is inaccurate or disputed.
