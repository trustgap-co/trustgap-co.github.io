These are estimates based on our current agreements; the deal link always shows the exact amounts, and those are what you pay.

Who pays, and what is refundable

Who pays the fees?

The seller chooses when creating the link, usually after agreeing with the buyer in chat: the buyer, the seller, or half each. Delivery is chosen separately the same way. The buyer sees exactly who pays what before paying.

If the seller never dispatches, what is refunded?

Everything. The buyer gets a full refund, including all fees and delivery.

If the item is wrong, fake or damaged?

If the dispute is upheld, the buyer gets a full refund to their own account and the item goes back to the seller. The cost of delivery and return falls on the seller.

If the buyer refuses a correct item at the door?

The item goes back to the seller. The cost of delivery and return may be deducted from the buyer’s refund, and every deduction is shown in writing.

Is payment processing refundable?

The payment provider does not return its processing charge when a payment is refunded. How that cost is shared in each refund case is set out in the Fees and Payments Policy, which is under legal review.

Are there any other charges?

No hidden charges. A failed or returned delivery may create a courier charge, handled as above. No charge is added after payment without being disclosed.
