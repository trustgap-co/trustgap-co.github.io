Why TrustGap exists

The hard part of social commerce is not finding the item. It is trusting what happens after you say yes.

Nigerian social commerce runs on conversations, referrals and small businesses. But a chat is not a payment guarantee, and a transfer receipt is not proof that the item will arrive. TrustGap adds the missing transaction layer, without asking anyone to leave WhatsApp or Instagram.

Buyers fear losing their money.
  • “If I transfer first, will the seller disappear?”
  • “If it’s fake or damaged, who will listen?”
Sellers fear losing the item and the money.
  • Buyers refusing to pay at the door.
  • Fake orders and wasted delivery fees.

How it works

Agree. Secure. Deliver. Inspect. Release.

Five clear steps from “we have a deal” to “everyone is satisfied”.

STEP 1

Agree

The seller turns the chat into one clear deal link: item, condition, price, delivery fee, who pays what, delivery address.

STEP 2

Secure

The buyer sees the exact deal and total, then pays. The money is held under the deal rules, not sent to the seller yet.

STEP 3

Deliver

Payment secured, the seller gets a clear go-ahead to dispatch. Delivery updates keep both sides informed.

STEP 4

Inspect

The buyer checks the item, then gives the rider a private delivery PIN. An inspection window follows for hidden problems.

STEP 5

Release

No valid issue raised? The payout follows the release rule. If there is an issue, the published dispute process applies.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does TrustGap replace WhatsApp or Instagram?

No. Keep the conversation where it started. TrustGap adds a protected deal link for the payment, delivery and release steps.

Does the seller get my money immediately?

No. The deal page shows the release condition. In the standard flow, the seller is paid after confirmed delivery and the stated inspection window, subject to the published dispute rules.

What if the item is wrong, damaged, fake or not as described?

Open a dispute within the stated inspection window and provide the requested evidence. TrustGap then follows the published review, return and refund rules.

Does TrustGap verify sellers?

TrustGap checks a seller’s identity (NIN) and that their payout account is in their own name. That does not mean TrustGap has independently verified every item, claim or business practice. Check the deal details and our policies before paying.
